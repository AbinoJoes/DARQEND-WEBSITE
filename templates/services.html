FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions lead to better solutions.

Have another question? ↗
What types of projects can Darqend discuss?

We can discuss websites, web applications, custom business software, API integrations, UI/UX design, and data-focused solutions. The available approach depends on the requirements.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes, you can contact us to discuss a business website or web application. Helpful details include your business goals, required pages, features, audience, and preferred timeline.

Can software be customized for our workflow?

Custom software can be planned around specific users, business processes, data, and features. The scope should be reviewed before development begins.

Do you work with existing applications?

You can contact us to discuss improvements, additional features, interface updates, or integrations for an existing application. The feasibility depends on the current system.

How do I get a project estimate?

Share your project description, key requirements, expected deliverables, and timeline through our contact page. The estimate depends on the agreed scope and complexity.

What information should I prepare before contacting you?

Start with the problem you want to solve, the intended users, important features, any existing system, and your expected timeline. You do not need to have every technical detail decided.
